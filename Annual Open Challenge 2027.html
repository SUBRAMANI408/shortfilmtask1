    Pay the fee at the desk on event day with your ID.

Questions

Where is my registration stored?

This page has no server. Registrations are saved in this browser on this device. Keep your registration ID and download the CSV to keep a copy.

Can I change my event?

Register again with the same name and the newer entry replaces the older one.

What if I'm on the age boundary?

Your age on the event date decides your category. Carry proof of age to the desk.
